## Pompes (`push_ups`)

| Rank | Subject (tone: piston / floor / endless pump) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | a nose almost touching a dark gym floor with two palms planted, minimalist style |
| Silver | a chrome piston pumping in a floor-plate cylinder, bold style |
| Gold | a towering wall of stacked push-up silhouettes receding into darkness, dramatic style |
| Platinum | a hydraulic jack lifting a glowing iron floor plate, luxurious style |
| Diamond | an eternal pump of molten iron looping through a circular heart of the earth, epic style |

## Tractions (`pull_ups`)

| Rank | Subject (tone: bar, lats, royalty of the hang) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | a chin just clearing a single dark pull-up bar, minimalist style |
| Silver | a V-taper back hanging from a bar with faint wing shadows, bold style |
| Gold | massive lats spreading like a cape under a glowing bar, dramatic style |
| Platinum | a bar addict chained affectionately to a chrome pull-up bar, luxurious style |
| Diamond | a crowned king seated on a pull-up bar throne above a dark gym, epic style |

## Squat poids du corps (`bw_squats`)

| Rank | Subject (tone: depth, well, no barbell) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | a figure squatting so deep they almost sit on grass, minimalist style |
| Silver | two soft-bent knees forming an arch over a dark pit, bold style |
| Gold | a bottomless stone well whose walls are repeating squat silhouettes, dramatic style |
| Platinum | an empty squat rack with no bar, only a glowing bodyweight figure, luxurious style |
| Diamond | an eternal well of light descending through the earth, epic style |

## Expert du poids du corps (`bw_expert`)

| Rank | Subject (tone: trinity, balance, no machines) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | three small icons — palms, a bar, a squat — in a tight triangle, minimalist style |
| Silver | a tightrope walker balancing a pull-up bar pole over a gym floor, bold style |
| Gold | a calisthenics athlete refusing a glowing weight machine that sits unused, dramatic style |
| Platinum | a marble calisthenist statue with three limbs becoming bar, floor, and squat, luxurious style |
| Diamond | a bodyweight expert haloed by a triangle of push pull squat constellations, epic style |

## 100 jours ferme (`hundred_a_day`)

| Rank | Subject (tone: French penal ladder — custody → hole → hundred-day sentence) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | a small holding-cell bench under a single bare bulb, garde à vue, minimalist style |
| Silver | a remand-cell door with a calendar of ten scratched days, bold style |
| Gold | a prison courtyard doing push-ups in formation for a one-month sentence, dramatic style |
| Platinum | a solitary mitard cell with a glowing hole in the floor, luxurious style |
| Diamond | a hundred tally marks carved in iron bars forming a crown, epic style |
